# Coffee Brewing ^os-schd01

Brewing extracts soluble compounds from ground coffee into water. ^os-scpar1

## Espresso ^os-schd02

- Uses finely ground coffee ^os-scbul1
- Forces hot water through at about 9 bars of pressure ^os-scbul2
- Extraction takes 25 to 30 seconds ^os-scbul3
- Produces a concentrated shot topped with crema ^os-scbul4

## Pour Over ^os-schd03

- Uses a medium-fine grind ^os-scbul5
- Water is poured in slow circular motions ^os-scbul6
- Total brew time is around three minutes ^os-scbul7

## French Press ^os-schd04

The simplest full-immersion brewing method. ^os-scpar2

- Coarse grounds steep directly in hot water ^os-scbul8
- A metal mesh plunger separates grounds from the brew ^os-scbul9
