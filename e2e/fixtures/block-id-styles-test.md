# Coffee Brewing

## Brewing Methods

- Pour over uses a slow spiral pour ^os-k3f9a2
- French press steeps grounds for four minutes ^os-m7q1x4
- Espresso forces water through at nine bars ^os-p2w8e6

## Grinder Types

- Burr grinders crush beans between two surfaces ^os-r5t0y3
- Blade grinders chop beans unevenly ^os-v9u4i7

## Water Notes

- Ideal brewing temperature is 90 to 96 degrees
- Filtered water avoids off-flavors from chlorine
