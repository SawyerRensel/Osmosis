# Coffee Brewing Methods ^os-tghd01

Grind size is the biggest lever on extraction. ^os-tgpar1

## Pour Over ^os-tghd02

- Use a medium-fine grind ^os-tgbul1
- Pour in slow concentric circles ^os-tgbul2
- Aim for a total brew time near three minutes ^tguser1

## French Press ^os-tghd03

- Use a coarse grind for full immersion
- Steep for four minutes before pressing
