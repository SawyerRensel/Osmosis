# Link Target

## Stock

- Choose straight-grained boards
- Let them acclimate for a week

## Joinery

- Mark out with a knife
### Dovetails

- Tails first, then pins
- Pare to the baseline

### Mortise and tenon

- Cut the mortise first

## Glue-up

- Dry fit everything
- Work in small batches ^glue-tip
- Clamp with even pressure

## Finishing

- Sand to 180 grit
- Wipe on the first coat of oil
