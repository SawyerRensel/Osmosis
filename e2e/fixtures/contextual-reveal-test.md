# Coffee Brewing Methods ^os-crhd01

Grind size is the main lever for extraction speed. ^os-crpar1

## Pour Over ^os-crhd02

- Use a medium-fine grind ^os-crbul1
- Pour in slow spirals from the center outward ^os-crbul2
	- Bloom with twice the coffee's weight in water first ^os-crbul3
- This bullet is untagged and must stay visible

## French Press ^os-crhd03

- Steep coarse grounds for four minutes ^os-crbul4
- Press slowly to avoid stirring up sediment ^os-crbul5

```text
ratio = water / coffee
target ratio: 16
```
^os-crcode

| Method | Brew time |
|---|---|
| Pour over | 3 min |
| French press | 4 min |
