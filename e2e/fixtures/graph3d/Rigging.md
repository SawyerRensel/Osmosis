# Rigging

## Standing Rigging

- Shrouds hold the mast sideways
- Forestay and backstay hold it fore and aft

## Running Rigging

- Halyards raise the sails
- Sheets trim the sails to the [[Weather]]

> [!tip] Tension check
> Sight up the mast before every trip.

```text
shroud tension: 12% of breaking load
forestay tension: 15% of breaking load
```
