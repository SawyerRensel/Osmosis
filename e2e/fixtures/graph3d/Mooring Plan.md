# Mooring Plan

The drag-to-link fixture for 3D editing slice 4. Open the 3D graph from this note, turn on Edit mode (pencil), and drag one line onto another. The harbour side is in [[Harbour Berths]].

## Lines and fenders

- Doubled bow line
- Spring line to the cleat ^spring-line
	- Check the chafe guard
- Fender boards on the quay side

## Tide table

> Springs: high water at noon
> Neaps: high water at six
> Always add a margin for the swell

## Departure checklist

- Slip the stern line first
- Hold the bow until the engine is warm
