# Deck Checklist

A heading with many sibling lines, short and long, to check that plates around one parent sit clear of each other. See [[Knots]] and [[Rigging]].

## Before casting off

- Fenders out
- Lines coiled
- Sheets led aft and free to run without kinks
- Halyards cleated
- Winch handles stowed in their pockets
- Bilge dry
- Fuel checked
- Water tanks topped up before every passage longer than a day
- Lifejackets on
- Jacklines rigged from bow to stern on both sides
- Radio on channel 16
- Navigation lights tested
- Anchor secured
- Hatches closed
- Boom vang eased so the boom can lift in a gust
- Reef lines ready
- Chart open
- Tide table checked against the harbour office board
- Weather forecast read
- Crew briefed on man-overboard drill
- Engine cooling water flowing
- Shore power unplugged
- Gangway stowed
- Mooring lines singled up, ready to slip from on board
