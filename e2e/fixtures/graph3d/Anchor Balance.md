# Anchor Balance

A note with its own 2D map settings: `direction: left-right` and `balance: alternating`. On a planet its continents deal their branches out to the left and right. It links back to [[Anchor Radial]] and on to [[Sailing Basics]], which has no settings of its own and follows the Overlays defaults.

## Night anchoring

- Show the anchor light
- Set an anchor alarm
	- Keep the radius small in a crowded bay
- Leave the engine ready

## Dragging

- Watch for the transit marks moving
- Let out more scope
- Re-set if it persists

## Crowded anchorage

- Keep clear of the swing circle
- Agree a plan with the neighbouring boats
- Fenders out when rafting up
