# Harbour Shapes

Every mind map topic shape, one line each; the lines with no shape of their own take the map's default (octagon).

## Shapes ^os-shp000

- Bollard (rect) ^os-shp001
- Cleat (rounded-rect) ^os-shp002
- Fender (pill) ^os-shp003
- Buoy (ellipse) ^os-shp004
- Lifebuoy (circle) ^os-shp005
- Channel marker (diamond) ^os-shp006
- Bitt (hexagon) ^os-shp007
- Stop sign at the slip (octagon) ^os-shp008
- Pennant (triangle) ^os-shp009
- Gangway (parallelogram) ^os-shp010
- Hull section (trapezoid) ^os-shp011
- Fog bank (cloud) ^os-shp012
- Leading light (arrow-right) ^os-shp013
- Waterline (underline) ^os-shp014
- Open water (none) ^os-shp015

## Borders ^os-shp100

- Dashed mooring line (rounded-rect, dashed) ^os-shp101
- Dotted chain (rounded-rect, dotted, 3 px) ^os-shp102
- Dashed buoy (ellipse, dashed) ^os-shp103
- Dotted marker (diamond, dotted) ^os-shp104
- Dashed fog (cloud, dashed) ^os-shp105
- Solid quay (rect, solid, 3 px) ^os-shp106
- No border (rounded-rect, none) ^os-shp107
- Dashed waterline (underline, dashed) ^os-shp108
- Outlined open water (none, with a border colour) ^os-shp109

## Map default ^os-shp200

- Slipway (no style: the map's octagon)
- Boathouse (no style: the map's octagon)

Compare with [[Styled Mooring]], whose unstyled lines stay rounded.
