# Watch Bill

Standing orders for the night watch.

## Deck

- Check the anchor light
- Log the wind ^log-wind
	- Gusts over twenty knots
	- Shift of more than forty degrees
- [ ] Wake the relief at four bells

## Galley

1. Fill the kettle
2. Light the stove
3. Stow the mugs

## Chart table

- Plot a fix every hour
	- Note the log reading
- Mark the traffic separation lane
