# Passage Plan

The flatten fixture: open its 3D graph and try the Overlays menu's Flatten branches slider. At the default, the numbered list below lies flat, 1 to 10 top to bottom beside its heading.

## Before casting off

1. Check the forecast
2. Fill the water tanks
3. Stow loose gear below
4. Test the bilge pump
5. Check the engine oil
6. Brief the crew on the route
7. Rig the jacklines
8. Hoist the mainsail at the dock
9. Single up the lines
10. Cast off from the bow

## Route

- Leave the harbour on the ebb
	- Pass the outer breakwater
		- Keep the green buoy to starboard
			- Read its light pattern in [[Navigation]]
- Cross the bay
	- Watch for ferries
