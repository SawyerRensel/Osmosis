# Rules of the Road

The relational study fixture (3D Graph Phase 6). Open the 3D graph from this note. It links to [[Crew Drills]], whose five drill cards are due too, and to [[Sailing Basics]].

## Who gives way

- Power gives way to sail, except in a narrow channel ^os-rr1
- A boat on port tack gives way to one on starboard tack ^os-rr2
- The overtaking boat keeps clear of the boat being overtaken ^os-rr3
- When in doubt, alter course early and obviously
