# Anchor Radial

A note with its own 2D map settings: `mapLayout: radial`. On a planet its continents fan round their headings instead of using the Overlays default. Next door is [[Anchor Balance]].

## Types of anchor

- Fluke anchor
	- Light and holds well in sand
- Plough anchor
- Claw anchor
- Mushroom anchor

## Rode

- Chain is heavy but resists chafe
- Rope stretches and absorbs shocks
- Scope of five to one in calm weather

## Setting the hook

- Motor slowly astern
- Take bearings on two fixed marks
- Check again after the tide turns

## Weighing anchor

- Motor up to the anchor
- Raise the chain by hand or windlass
- Wash off the mud before it comes aboard
