# Crew Drills

The Overlays menu fixture (3D Graph Phase 5). One hop from [[Sailing Basics]], and it links to [[Crew Roster]], which doesn't exist.

## Drills on the water #drills

- Tacking drill: helm calls "ready about", crew sheets in ^os-drill1
- Man overboard drill: point, shout, throw the horseshoe buoy ^os-drill2
- Reefing drill: ease the halyard, hook the cringle ^os-drill3
- Heaving to: back the jib, lash the helm to leeward ^os-drill4
- Anchoring drill: pay out five times the depth ^os-drill5
- Fender drill, with no card

## Safety briefing #safety

1. Lifejackets on before leaving the dock
2. Show the crew the fire extinguisher

> A drill is only learned once it is done without thinking.

Every drill ends with a short debrief on deck.
