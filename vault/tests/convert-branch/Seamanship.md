# Seamanship

## Rigging

Lines and wires that hold up the mast and work the sails.

### Standing rigging

- Shrouds brace the mast sideways ^os-shr001
  - Upper shrouds
  - Lower shrouds
- Stays brace it fore and aft

### Running rigging

- Halyards raise the sails
- Sheets trim the sails
