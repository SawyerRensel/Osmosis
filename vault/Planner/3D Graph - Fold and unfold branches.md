# Feature Request

An idea Sawyer raised in the Batch 1 test, 2026-10-31. Not yet designed.

## What do you need built?

A way to fold (collapse) and unfold branches in the 3D graph, the way branches collapse and expand in the 2D mind map view. Possibly they unfold automatically as you "walk the line" toward them.

## Questions to settle before building

- Does folding in 3D share fold state with the 2D map (same file), or is it separate?
- Auto-unfold on approach vs. explicit toggle; how it interacts with streaming (Phase 3) and the near tier.
- What a folded branch looks like (a badge with the hidden count on the plate?).
